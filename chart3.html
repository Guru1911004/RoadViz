Fines by Region: What Can a Map Tell Us?

This map visualisation uncovers the geographic footprint of speed enforcement across Australia in 2023, measured as fines per 1,000 licensed drivers. What begins as a straightforward heatmap quickly reveals deeper behavioural and policy-driven contrasts between states.

Click a state/territory to explore the spatial distribution.

Key Regional Observations

  • Western Australia emerges as a clear outlier, with more than 400 fines per 1,000 licenses — significantly above all other jurisdictions. This intensity may reflect stricter local policies, a smaller licensing population, or targeted enforcement in high-risk corridors. In contrast, regions like South Australia and Tasmania report much lower per-capita fines, potentially indicating different enforcement strategies or traffic conditions.
  • Eastern states such as Victoria and New South Wales show moderate yet consistent enforcement, likely driven by urban congestion, dense road networks, and automated camera systems. Queensland’s decentralised road network may explain its relatively balanced fine density.
  • Queensland presents balanced enforcement, possibly due to its wide, decentralised road network.

    • By mapping fines relative to driver population, rather than absolute totals, this view emphasizes equity and enforcement efficiency, helping stakeholders ask: Where are road rules most aggressively policed — and why?

    Conclusion

    This spatial analysis highlights how enforcement varies not just in volume, but in its relationship to the driving population. It encourages a shift in how we evaluate road safety — not merely by counting fines, but by asking how fairly and consistently they're distributed.

    By identifying outliers and trends across states, this map provides a starting point for further investigation into policy effectiveness, behavioural drivers, and infrastructure design. In doing so, it empowers policymakers, researchers, and the public to better understand the regional nuances behind Australia’s speed enforcement landscape.

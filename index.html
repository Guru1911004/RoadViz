Explore Age-Based Speeding Trends Across States (2023)

Ever wondered which age group racks up the most speeding fines — and why? Are younger drivers more reckless, or do older drivers spend more time behind the wheel?

Using our interactive features like colored state buttons , interactive tooltips and the dynamic live summary, you can use the dashboard to help explore speeding data across Australian states from 2023 and uncover patterns to how different age groups are penalised across states.

Note: Fines issued to drivers aged 0–16 are extremely low across all jurisdictions — likely because most in this group are underage or learner drivers under supervision. To avoid visual distortion, this group is excluded from the chart. However, a dynamic summary below the graph updates in real time to show their total fines per state.


Fines by Age Group in 2023 (select a state to view the stats)



  • Interestingly, states such as the the Australian Capital Territory, Northern Territory, and Tasmania consistently recorded lower fine totals across ages compared to other regions. This may reflect their smaller populations, fewer major traffic corridors, or limited use of automated enforcement technologies.

  • Across all jurisdictions, individuals aged 40–64 consistently recorded the highest number of speeding fines in 2023, likely due to commuting, household duties, or longer-distance travel between cities and regions. Their frequent driving — whether for work or personal life — possibly contributes to their greater exposure and higher infringement rates.
